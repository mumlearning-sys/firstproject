# CASA

## 80-SECOND PRODUCT LAUNCH FILM

**Product:** CASA — Conversational Agentic System for Analytics
**Total duration:** 80 seconds
**Structure:** 4 Acts / 8 Scenes
**Format:** 16:9 widescreen
**Style:** Premium enterprise technology launch film
**Human presence:** Act 1 only
**Narration:** One consistent off-screen narrator throughout
**Character dialogue:** None
**Brand reveal:** Final scene only

---

# 1. LOCKED STORY

### ACT 1 — THE PROBLEM

**0–20 seconds**

Customer problem → transaction declined → mobile banking app → initiate chat → type question.

### ACT 2 — THE SCALE

**20–45 seconds**

One conversation → millions of conversations → AI-ready data.

### ACT 3 — THE INTELLIGENCE

**45–65 seconds**

Reporting/monitoring → natural-language question → billing address mismatch.

### ACT 4 — THE PRODUCT

**65–80 seconds**

Enrich → Monitor → Explore → CASA.

The Streamlit scene must be treated as a **cinematic moment**, not a screen recording.

**QUESTION → SYSTEM THINKS → ANSWER → INSIGHT**

not:

**QUESTION → READ A LONG AI RESPONSE**

### Visible

brief processing
