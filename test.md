# Product Launch Video — Complete Storyboard

**Working title:**

### **From Conversations to Intelligence**

**Hero message:**

### **The customer changed channels. The problem didn't.**

**Target duration:** ~3–4 minutes

**Primary format:** 16:9 cinematic product film

**Tone:** Premium, intelligent, human, sophisticated, trustworthy, enterprise-grade

**Core story:**
A customer asks the virtual assistant why a transaction was declined. What initially looks like a simple digital interaction becomes an investigation across millions of conversations, customer segments, and contact-center interactions. The platform discovers that approximately 50% of these customers subsequently contact the contact center, identifies the leading reason, follows the customer across channels, and reconstructs the complete journey.

# 3. What Should NOT Be Used

For the marketing/video version:

**Do not use:**

* Customer names
* Account numbers
* Card numbers
* Phone numbers
* Email addresses
* Real PII
* Unapproved raw conversations
* Internal credentials
* Production database details
* Sensitive operational information

The **business insight can be real**, while the visual representation can be sanitized.

---

# 4. Real Data vs Visual Representation

This distinction is important for the video team.

### Business facts

Should be **real and validated**.

For example:

> Approximately 50% of declined-transaction inquiry customers subsequently contacted the contact center.

### Visual conversations

Can be:

> **Sanitized / anonymized / representative**

provided they faithfully represent the type of interaction and don't introduce false business claims.

### UI

Can use:

> Actual application screenshots + recreated animation states

This gives us the best combination of **credibility + visual quality + data privacy**.

---

# 5. Production Guidance for Video Developer

The video developer should **not simply screen-record the application**.

Use a combination of:

### 40–50%

Actual application UI

### 20–30%

Cinematic customer/banking visuals

### 15–20%

Data/AI visualization

### 10–15%

Architecture/intelligence visualization

This will make it feel like a **product launch film**, rather than an internal application demo.

---

# 6. Critical Direction to the Video Team

The story should progressively answer:

> **What happened?**

then:

> **Why did it happen?**

then:

> **Where did the customer go?**

then:

> **What was the underlying problem?**

then:

> **How was it resolved?**

and finally:

> **How did the platform discover all of this?**

That progression is what makes the film compelling.

The technology should be **revealed after the business value**, not before it.

---

## The single most important storytelling principle

Don't start with:

> "Our platform uses multiple AI agents to analyze banking conversations."

Start with:

> **"A customer asks: Why was my transaction declined?"**

Then let the audience discover the intelligence **through the investigation**.

Only after they've seen the result should we reveal the sophisticated platform powering it.

That will make the video much more powerful for both **leadership and the eventual marketing audience**.
